The International Fission Yeast Conference is co-organized by Maitreyi Das at Boston College this year! It's really nice to get to see Maitrayi again. Last time we met was at the Triangle cytoskeleton meeting in 2017 or something. I was still at Duke for PhD and Maitreyi's lab was still at the University of Tennessee back then. Wen-Han, Me (Jian-geng) and I-Ju went together. The conference was a lot of fun and it's nice to see how the fission yeast community being tight and cozy.

Wen-Han shares a poster on our recent progress of fission yeast polarity dynamics and how its witinin-population variation influences our mechanistic view of how it's regulated. We had some nice experience exchange with Maitreyi as well as Sophie Martin at University of Geneva and Fulvia Verde at the University of Miami. The ball was fun, but both Wen-Han and I-Ju insisted in just being photographers.

I also got to visit Chi-Fang from my PhD lab and Danny my PhD advisor, and see how Danny's lab moves to discover some cool cell biology of the dimorphic <i>Aureobasidium pullulans </i>. Some of those movies really got us realizing how evolution can re-route a bunch of conserved genes we're familiar with in the budding yeast context to do crazy things that I can't imagine.
